05 / RADIO LABORATORY

SETI narrowband detector

EXPERIMENTAL

Autonomous file-watching analysis

For the SETI channel, drop a real Breakthrough Listen .fil or .h5 spectrogram (or a small .npz / .npy power matrix) into data/inbox/. SIGNALWATCH will inspect new files on the next monitoring cycle, flag possible drifting narrowband traces, and record them as unverified candidates.

Real telescope recordings require the optional blimpy package. Local datasets are analyzed; this does not currently tap a continuous live SETI telescope feed. Separately, the e-Callisto solar channel now retrieves recently posted radio observations automatically (when enabled).
